Data notes

Before you read too far into it

  • Meta covers Nov 2 to Nov 8 in the ad account's timezone. Apple covers Nov 3 to Nov 9 in UTC. The windows are offset by a day.
  • Meta can take up to 3 days to report iOS installs, so the last few days may still go up.
  • Apple counts 7 redownloads in its 78 installs. First time downloads from Apple are 71.
  • Active user data isn't included. AppsFlyer no longer provides Ivy conversion data.
